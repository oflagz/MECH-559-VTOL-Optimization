# Independent source data
All input numerical observations below were transcribed for this fresh study from the **UIUC Propeller Data Site, Volume 1, version 3 (2015)**, originally measured by J. Brandt and M. Selig. Geometry is the published approximate digitization of APC Slow Flyer 10 x 7.

- `apcsf_10x7_geometry.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_geom.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_static.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_static_kt0827.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_3008.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_kt0828_3008.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_4011.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_kt0829_4011.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_5003.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_kt0831_5003.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_5006.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_kt0832_5006.txt
- `apcsf_10x7_6014.csv`: https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/volume-1/data/apcsf_10x7_kt0834_6014.txt

Reference: Brandt, J.B., and Selig, M.S., "Propeller Performance Data at Low Reynolds Numbers," AIAA Paper 2011-1255.

These are measurements on **one 0.254 m diameter, two-bladed propeller** and are inadequate to demonstrate empirical accuracy when extrapolated to a 0.4-0.6m or 3-/4-bladed geometry. The purpose is benchmark checking of the new physics model, not validation of a different manufactured rotor.
