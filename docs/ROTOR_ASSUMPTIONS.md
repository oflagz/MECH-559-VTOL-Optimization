# Rotor model assumptions

- Rotor pitch at 75% radius: 20° fixed; not manufacturer pitch geometry.
- Rotor mass scaling extrapolates APC 10×7 and 11×7 product masses.
- Wing mass, drag breakdown and download fraction come from Antoine's individual report.
- Propulsion mass and 382 Wh usable allowance come from Damian's individual report.
- Remaining airframe and nacelle masses are assumptions.
- Drive efficiency is fixed at 85%; motor voltage is checked separately.
- Validity limits on section incidence and cruise advance ratio restrict model extrapolation.
- Interval results are deterministic scenarios, not confidence limits.
